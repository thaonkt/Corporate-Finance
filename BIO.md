# Nguyen Khong Thanh Thao

**Marketing Manager, Commercial Segment | Lenovo Vietnam**

Nguyen Khong Thanh Thao is a commercial marketing professional with 10+ years of cross-industry experience spanning technology, consulting, media, and B2B/B2C marketing. She currently serves as Marketing Manager for the Commercial Segment at Lenovo Vietnam, driving marketing and communications strategy across the full ThinkPad, ThinkBook, ThinkStation, ThinkCentre, ThinkVision, and Accessories portfolio — with a focus on solutions-led growth, customer acquisition, retention, partner development, and demand generation.

Her expertise covers product and solution marketing, digital and social strategy, partner marketing, integrated communications, event strategy, campaign optimization, and customer journey planning. Prior to Lenovo, Thao developed cross-functional capabilities at Microsoft Vietnam, Michael Page, ABB Vietnam, and Saatchi & Saatchi Vietnam across OEM partnerships, channel readiness, industrial marketing, and brand communications.

Thao holds a Bachelor of Business Administration from International University, VNU-HCMC. She is a former President of the IU VNU-HCMC Alumni Association, former President of the External Relations Department of the International University Youth Union, and a Top 30 Finalist in the P&G ASEAN Business Challenge.

Her goal is to deepen her impact at the intersection of technology, business strategy, customer insight, and measurable growth — translating strategic vision into campaigns that drive real commercial outcomes.
